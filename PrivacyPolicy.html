01

Who we are

The Ahmadiyya Quran Translations app (“the App”, shown on your device as “Quran”) is a free app for Android and iOS. It is developed independently by Noman Ahmed (“the developer”, “we”) in Moncton, New Brunswick, Canada. The developer is responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

The App is not an official publication of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Jamā‘at and is not operated by it.

02

Information that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the App’s private storage on your device. It is never sent to the developer:

  • bookmarks and your last-read position;
  • recent searches;
  • your chosen translations, text sizes, commentary display and theme;
  • the Quran text and the translations you download.

It is deleted when you clear the App’s data or uninstall it. If your device backs up app data (for example Android backup to your Google account, or iCloud backup), that backup may include this information. The backup is held under your own account and that provider’s terms.

03

Information sent to other services

The App uses the services below. None of them receives your name, email address, contacts or the text you type into search.

Google Analytics for Firebase

Usage statistics · provided by Google

What
A random app-instance ID; the screens you open; how long you use the App; events such as which translation you downloaded, which display settings you turned on and which surah you opened from search results; app version, device model, operating system, language and approximate region (worked out by Google from your IP address). On Android, Google may also receive the device’s advertising ID. The App does not use it for advertising.
Why
To see which features are used and where to improve.
Kept
Up to 14 months, then deleted by Google.
Policy
Firebase privacy · Google privacy policy

Firebase Crashlytics

Crash and error reports · provided by Google

What
When the App crashes or hits an error: a technical stack trace, short diagnostic messages, app version, device model, operating system, free memory and storage, and a random installation ID. Reports never include verse text, your searches or data returned by servers.
Why
To find and fix bugs.
Kept
90 days.
Policy
Firebase privacy

Firebase Performance Monitoring

Speed measurements · provided by Google

What
How long the App takes to start and to complete tasks such as downloads; for its network requests, the address pattern, response time, size and status code; plus device model, operating system, app version and a random installation ID.
Why
To keep the App fast and spot slow downloads.
Kept
A limited period set by Google.
Policy
Firebase privacy

Open Quran API

Translation downloads · api.openquran.com

What
When you download a translation, the App requests its chapters from this service. Like any web server, it receives your IP address, the chapter and translation requested and standard technical details of the request. The App sends no identifier with these requests.
Why
To deliver the translation you asked for.
Kept
According to that service’s own practices.

Google Play and the Apple App Store

Installing and updating the App

What
The store you installed from handles your download and updates. It gives the developer aggregated statistics such as install counts and crash rates, which do not identify you.
Policy
Google privacy policy · Apple privacy policy

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

04

How information is used

Information from the services above is used only to:

  • find, diagnose and fix crashes and errors;
  • understand which features and translations are used, to decide what to improve next;
  • keep downloads and the App fast and reliable.

It is not used for advertising or for profiling you, and the developer does not try to link it to your identity.

Legal basis. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, this processing relies on the developer’s legitimate interest in keeping the App working and improving it. Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), you consent by using the App. You can withdraw that consent at any time, as described in Your choices and rights.

05

Sharing and disclosure

Information is not sold, rented or shared for cross-context behavioural advertising. It is handled only by the service providers listed above, which act on the developer’s behalf. It may also be disclosed:

  • if required by law, such as a court order or subpoena;
  • if necessary to protect the safety, rights or property of users, the developer or others.

International transfers. Google processes Firebase data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, Google relies on safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

06

How long it is kept

  • On your device: until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.
  • Analytics: up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Performance data: a limited period set by Google.

Aggregated reports that cannot identify anyone may be kept longer.

07

Your choices and rights

Limit what is collected

  • Advertising ID (Android): delete or reset it in your device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Stop all collection: uninstall the App. Nothing further is sent after that.
  • Remove local data: clear the App’s storage in your device settings, or uninstall it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to receive a copy of it. Because the App has no accounts, the developer usually cannot tell which data belongs to you. Email the developer and they will explain what can be done. Where possible, they will use Google’s tools to delete analytics data linked to your device.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Canada, that is the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

08

Children

The App is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, email the developer and it will be deleted where possible.

09

Security

Everything the App sends over the internet uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Data on your device is kept in the App’s private storage, which the operating system protects. The developer runs no servers of their own; Firebase data is held by Google under its security programme. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your device updated and protected with a screen lock.
